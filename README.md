# Flow reconfiguration

## What is in this repository?

- The [Lean directory](lean/) contains the statements, unconditional Lean 4 proofs (from Lean's three standard axioms alone), and verification instructions for integer, modular, and abelian-group flow reconfiguration (Theorems 1.1–1.3 and all supporting lemmas and remarks) and for the two published theorems that the paper quotes (Theorem 1.4, the 6-flow theorem of Seymour and Tutte; Theorem 3.8, the well-colouring lemma of Jaeger, Linial, Payan, and Tarsi).
- The [comparator directory](comparator/) contains the independent minimal-mode challenge statements and configuration for comparing them against the proof library with `lake comparator`.
